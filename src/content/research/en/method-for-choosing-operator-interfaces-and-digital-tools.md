whyItMatters: "Digitalization projects usually start from the available technology and only later ask what the operator needs. This method reverses the order and offers a path that engineers with no background in human factors can follow. The study is not about cobots: it focuses on the interfaces and devices of the warehouse operator, and the link to collaborative cells is the reader's."
method: "The method is inspired by the House of Quality from quality function deployment (QFD). After questionnaires, focus groups, personas and scenarios, five matrices are chained together: sustainability goals and user skills; skills and tool functions; interaction requirements and interaction modes; modes and interface types; available data and adaptive interface behaviors. Experts assign the correlations on a scale of 0, 1, 3 and 9, and the company sets the weight of each item. The authors applied the method to a logistics case, with an automated warehouse and maintenance, in remote focus groups with company employees."
findings:
  - "A panel of experts selected 13 sustainability and circular economy goals from the literature. In the case studied, the top score went to the operator's awareness of the machine and process, upskilling and reskilling, and better safety conditions."
  - "The focus group described work with frequent and variable communications, large operating areas, noise and constant use of glasses and helmet."
  - "The most recommended functions were process information (15.91%), product information, including the digital product passport (13.47%), and step-by-step guidance (12.63%)."
  - "Among interface types, the smartwatch (17.94%) and augmented reality glasses (14.95%) came ahead of monitor, tablet and smartphone, tied at 10.96%."
  - "The most recommended adaptive behaviors were organizing information according to the operator's profile (10.05%) and giving suggestions (9.37%). The authors recommend wearable devices, which give access to data in different areas of the warehouse during the shift."
limitations: "The model is linear and simplified, and the correlations were assigned by experts from the project itself. The method was applied to a single case, with confidential data, and the suggested interfaces were not evaluated with users. The study also foresees using the operator's physiological data to adapt the interface, without discussing privacy or acceptance."
