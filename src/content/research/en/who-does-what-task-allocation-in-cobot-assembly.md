whyItMatters: "Deciding what goes to the person and what goes to the robot shapes cycle time, ergonomics and even whether the work will be monotonous or stressful. The review organizes the options and shows what tends to be left out of the decision, such as safety, which helps anyone planning a collaborative cell."
method: "The authors carried out a systematic review with a search in January 2022 in the Scopus and IEEE Xplore databases, restricted to peer-reviewed texts in English or German. Of the 576 records, three rounds of screening left 24 highly relevant studies, and searching references and citations added 13, for a total of 37. The approaches were classified into two categories and four subcategories, defined in discussion among the authors."
findings:
  - "The review divides the approaches into static ones, which decide before execution (19 studies), and dynamic ones, which decide during execution (18). Static approaches rely on assessing the suitability of each task (14) or on simulation (5); dynamic ones are reactive (12) or proactive (6)."
  - "The decision criteria form 12 groups. In the 30 approaches analyzed for criteria, the characteristics of the person and the robot appear in all of them, process requirements in 87%, time in 70% and ergonomics in 47%; safety appears in only 8. Ergonomics covers almost only posture and physical effort."
  - "The choice and weighting of criteria are rarely validated in practice or derived from standards. Almost all approaches were evaluated in the laboratory, and real deployments and long-term studies are lacking."
  - "In proactive allocation, the person leads and the robot anticipates supporting tasks. The review found a gain in autonomy and less monotony, at the cost of lower efficiency, unpredictable cycle times and a return on investment that is harder to calculate."
  - "The authors propose a reference model with up to seven steps, from input criteria to monitoring with feedback. They recommend static allocation for large batches and standardized processes and dynamic allocation for less standardized processes; the latter requires sensors, real-time computing and care with the privacy of worker data."
limitations: "The search goes only up to January 2022 and used two databases. Because almost all the studies reviewed were tested in the laboratory, the review does not show how these approaches behave in factories, and the reference model is the authors' proposal, not yet validated. Standardized measures for comparing the approaches with one another are also lacking."
