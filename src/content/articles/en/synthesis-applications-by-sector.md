Cobots already appear from welding to the laboratory, but the evidence is uneven. In the 16 summaries this synthesis draws on, almost nothing was measured with a collaborative arm in production: concepts, simulations, reviews, and lab tests dominate. In several sectors the robot studied is not even an arm, but a vehicle, a construction machine, or a service robot.

## In the factory, the question is who does each task

Neither of the two manufacturing studies measured a cell in operation. In a welding cell concept, the welder takes the short, complex seams and the robot takes the long, straight ones; the robot welds only when the operator is outside the area ([Müller et al., 2017](/en/research/skill-based-task-allocation-human-robot-welding/)). In an optimization model for assembly lines, tested on 164 configurations, including ergonomics cut the risk indicators by about a quarter. Adding robots showed diminishing returns: in one line, the fourth robot brought no gain ([Kulaç & Kiraz, 2026](/en/research/assembly-line-balancing-with-cobots-and-ergonomic-risk/)).

## In the warehouse, the robot is a vehicle and acceptance matters

A review of 36 warehouse studies found mostly guided vehicles and mobile robots. Productivity gains in order picking depend on planning and a good division of tasks ([Jacob et al., 2023](/en/research/acceptance-of-robot-colleagues-in-warehouses-review/)). In an experiment with 60 students in a laboratory warehouse, picking orders with a mobile robot raised satisfaction compared with manual picking ([Pasparakis et al., 2023](/en/research/order-picking-with-a-robot-raises-job-satisfaction/)). With an autonomous order-picking truck, planning the route together with the stretches on foot cut travel time by 17.4% on average, on real pick lists from a distribution center ([Tutam & De Koster, 2024](/en/research/walk-or-ride-order-picking-with-autonomous-vehicle/)).

## On the construction site, factory rules are not enough

A review of 287 studies concluded that the ISO 10218 standard is insufficient for the job site; in that review, the collaborative arm is a minority next to teleoperated machines and exoskeletons ([Lin et al., 2025](/en/research/human-robot-collaboration-safety-construction-review/)). A taxonomy of 16 categories provides vocabulary to describe human roles, autonomy, and safety ([Rodrigues et al., 2023](/en/research/taxonomy-human-robot-interaction-in-construction/)). Another paper, in which cobot means construction machines, warns that automating an isolated task can cut the informal exchange of information on site ([Schöberl et al., 2023](/en/research/collaborative-machines-on-site-automation-and-collaboration/)). In the only construction test with a collaborative arm, ten participants with an architecture background previewed a cobot's motion in augmented reality, and the cobot still collided several times with the structure ([Loy et al., 2025](/en/research/augmented-reality-exploratory-assembly-with-a-cobot/)). In virtual reality, with 30 people, separating the work areas of a person and a masonry robot increased perceived safety ([You et al., 2018](/en/research/perceived-safety-construction-robot-virtual-reality/)). A proposal to generate instructions for people and robots with artificial intelligence did not reach execution ([Das et al., 2026](/en/research/ai-translates-construction-drawings-for-humans-and-robots/)).

## In the field and the meat plant, the product is never the same

A review of 32 studies on mobile agricultural machines found that the human-robot partnership improved fruit detection in every case compared, but most of the evidence comes from simulation ([Benos et al., 2023](/en/research/human-robot-interaction-in-agriculture-systematic-review/)). On a farm, wearable sensors on 20 participants recognized six steps of handing a crate to a stationary agricultural vehicle, with a mean accuracy of 85.6% ([Anagnostis et al., 2021](/en/research/wearable-sensors-recognize-farm-worker-activities/)). In meat cutting, a narrative review shows that the cobot's force limiting does not protect when the tool is a knife; the cell it proposes was not built ([Romanov et al., 2022](/en/research/cobots-in-meat-processing-challenges-and-possibilities/)).

## In healthcare, the map is wide and the test is small

A narrative review of 47 studies describes mostly service, surgical, and rehabilitation robots, and names cost, privacy, and liability for harm as open issues ([Weerarathna et al., 2023](/en/research/robots-in-healthcare-applications-barriers-narrative-review/)). In a microbiology laboratory, a cobot taught by demonstration picked 56 bacterial colonies, and about 11% of the samples were not identified. Deposition taught by hand was replaced by a programmed trajectory ([Baumkircher et al., 2022](/en/research/cobot-picks-bacterial-colonies-in-the-laboratory/)).

## What is still unknown

The studies with people use small samples or students, not workers from the sector ([Pasparakis et al., 2023](/en/research/order-picking-with-a-robot-raises-job-satisfaction/); [You et al., 2018](/en/research/perceived-safety-construction-robot-virtual-reality/); [Anagnostis et al., 2021](/en/research/wearable-sensors-recognize-farm-worker-activities/)). Several gains are stated without measurement, such as efficiency in welding and the speed-up in earthworks ([Müller et al., 2017](/en/research/skill-based-task-allocation-human-robot-welding/); [Schöberl et al., 2023](/en/research/collaborative-machines-on-site-automation-and-collaboration/)). The reviews point to a lack of field studies in warehouses and of validation on real construction sites ([Jacob et al., 2023](/en/research/acceptance-of-robot-colleagues-in-warehouses-review/); [Lin et al., 2025](/en/research/human-robot-collaboration-safety-construction-review/)). Several studies include no risk assessment ([Baumkircher et al., 2022](/en/research/cobot-picks-bacterial-colonies-in-the-laboratory/); [Loy et al., 2025](/en/research/augmented-reality-exploratory-assembly-with-a-cobot/)).

## In practice

- Check which robot the study used: in warehouses, construction, and farming, the term cobot often covers vehicles and machines, not arms ([Jacob et al., 2023](/en/research/acceptance-of-robot-colleagues-in-warehouses-review/); [Lin et al., 2025](/en/research/human-robot-collaboration-safety-construction-review/); [Benos et al., 2023](/en/research/human-robot-interaction-in-agriculture-systematic-review/)).
- In a line, start with repetitive or high-risk tasks; more robots do not always bring more gain ([Kulaç & Kiraz, 2026](/en/research/assembly-line-balancing-with-cobots-and-ergonomic-risk/)).
- With a dangerous tool, do not rely only on the cobot's force limiting: it does not protect against the knife ([Romanov et al., 2022](/en/research/cobots-in-meat-processing-challenges-and-possibilities/)).
