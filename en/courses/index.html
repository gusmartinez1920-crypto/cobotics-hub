Courses

Collaborative robotics courses that already exist, offered by manufacturers, open platforms, schools and universities. A short list so you know where to start.

Cobotics Hub has no ties to these providers and receives nothing for listing them. Only courses whose page we could open and check are included; prices, languages and schedules change, so confirm on each provider's site. Written with AI assistance and still under review by the team.

Manufacturer training

  • Universal Robots

    UR Academy — Free e-Learning

    Partly free, partly paid Language: Several languages

    Interactive, self-paced online modules with simulation: programming the brand's cobots and a module on risk assessment. It is the most complete free material we found. The company also offers paid in-person courses.

    Open the course page →

  • FANUC America

    CRX e-Learning

    Cost not stated Language: English

    A path of short videos for beginners on the CRX line of cobots. When we checked, only some of the videos had been published.

    Open the course page →

  • Doosan Robotics

    Robot Training (Levels 1–3)

    Cost not stated Language: Not stated

    Three levels (elementary, intermediate and advanced) at official training centers, with a certificate, plus a link to online training.

    Open the course page →

  • Omron

    TMflow Programming Level 1

    Cost not stated Language: Not stated

    A beginner course on programming TM cobots, in person or online on demand.

    Open the course page →

  • igus (RBTX Academy)

    DOBOT Studio — Basic Training

    Paid Language: Not stated

    Hands-on training of about one day, in small groups, on the low-cost robots sold through the platform.

    Open the course page →

Open online courses

  • Coursera · University at Buffalo (SUNY)

    Collaborative Robot Safety: Design & Deployment

    Partly free, partly paid Language: English, with subtitles in several languages

    A beginner course, in four modules and about 16 hours, on safety in designing and deploying cobots. It is the only open, vendor-neutral safety course we found. The certificate is paid.

    Open the course page →

  • Coursera · L&T EduTech

    Collaborative Robotics in Industry (Specialization)

    Paid Language: English

    An intermediate specialization with three courses and about 26 hours. Despite the name, the course titles cover robotics and automation in general.

    Open the course page →

In Portuguese and Spanish

  • SENAI (Brasil)

    Robótica Colaborativa Aplicada

    Paid Language: Portuguese

    A low-cost, 40-hour distance course offered by SENAI's state units. It belongs to a set of Industry 4.0 courses launched in 2020. The link goes to the page of SENAI in Rio Grande do Norte, where the course was checked.

    Open the course page →

  • Universal Robots Brasil

    Curso de robótica colaborativa na UR

    Partly free, partly paid Language: Portuguese

    A page in Portuguese explaining the brand's training in Brazil: the free online academy and the paid in-person courses.

    Open the course page →

  • IMH (Elgoibar, Espanha)

    Robótica colaborativa. Instalación y programación

    Free Language: Spanish

    A 24-hour in-person course on installing and programming cobots, subsidized for participants in the edition we checked.

    Open the course page →

Universities

  • Free University of Bozen-Bolzano (Itália)

    Industrial Collaborative Robotics

    Cost not stated Language: English

    A 6-credit course in the MSc in Industrial Mechanical Engineering. One module covers safety and ergonomics in human-robot interaction and the other covers applications.

    Open the course page →

  • Arts et Métiers (França)

    Mastère Spécialisé — Expert in collaborative robotics for the industry of the future (ColRobot)

    Cost not stated Language: French

    A postgraduate specialization dedicated to collaborative robotics in industry. The link goes to the ParisTech network page, where the program was checked.

    Open the course page →

What is still missing

  • Almost everything is programming training from one specific manufacturer, in English.
  • There is little vendor-neutral content in Portuguese and Spanish, and we found no course specific to Latin America in Spanish.
  • We found no course linking cobots with industrial IoT and digital twins, and none aimed at small companies deciding whether and how to adopt a cobot.
  • Human factors and ergonomics appear only as part of one university course.

Pages checked on November 13, 2026.
