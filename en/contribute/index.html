Ground rules

  • Summaries are written in your own words. Do not paste the abstract.
  • Always cite the original with DOI or a stable link.
  • Upload a PDF only if its license allows redistribution (for example CC BY).
  • Disclose any link with the manufacturers or authors you write about.
