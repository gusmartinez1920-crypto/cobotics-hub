Privacy notice

Cobotics Hub is a free knowledge site. This notice explains which personal data we handle and why. We collect as little as possible.

Browsing the site

The site uses no first-party cookies, no audience analytics and no advertising. Fonts are served by the site itself, with no calls to third-party services.

Like any web server, our hosting provider (Hostinger) may log technical access data, such as IP address, date and page visited, for the security and operation of the service.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, we process your email address (and name, if you provide it) only to send the newsletter, based on your consent.

Sign-up and sending are handled by Hostinger Reach, acting on our behalf. The sign-up form may use the tool's own cookies or similar technologies to work.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to delete your data at any time.

Contributions via GitHub

Summaries and proposals sent through the Contribute page are stored publicly on GitHub and are subject to GitHub's privacy policy. When we publish a contribution, we show the name you give us.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw your newsletter consent. These rights are provided, for example, by the LGPD (Brazil) and the GDPR (European Union).
