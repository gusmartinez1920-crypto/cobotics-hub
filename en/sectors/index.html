Applications by sector

Where cobots, industrial IoT and digital twins are used in each industry — and what to watch out for.

Cobotics Industrial IoT Digital twin

Automotive

Assembly, screwdriving and inspection next to people, on lines with many vehicle variants.

Typical applications

  • Screwdriving with torque and angle control
  • Applying adhesives and sealants
  • Visual inspection of finish and fit
  • Machine tending (loading and unloading parts)

SDG 8 SDG 9

Cobotics Industrial IoT Digital twin

Electronics

Testing, inspection and handling of boards and small products, in varied batches and with care against electrostatic discharge.

Typical applications

  • Loading and unloading boards into test fixtures
  • Optical inspection of boards and assemblies
  • Pick-and-place of larger components and connectors
  • Screwdriving small enclosures

SDG 9 SDG 12

Cobotics Industrial IoT Digital twin

Food and beverage

Packing, palletizing and quality control, with specific hygiene and cleaning requirements.

Typical applications

  • Placing packaged products into cases (secondary packaging)
  • End-of-line palletizing
  • Loading and unloading packaging machines
  • Visual inspection of labels, caps and seals

SDG 8 SDG 12

Logistics and warehousing

Palletizing, order picking and mobile robots with a collaborative arm, in warehouses and distribution centers.

Typical applications

  • Palletizing and depalletizing cases
  • Item picking at picking stations
  • Kitting parts for production
  • Loading and unloading conveyors
